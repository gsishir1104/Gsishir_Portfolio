Phantom Escape: Enchanted Maze

Project overview

A third-person Unity game focused on exploring a maze, solving puzzles, and avoiding enemies. My programming work focused on enemy patrol, player detection, and pursuit behavior.

Gameplay programming

  • Enemy patrol logic: Implemented enemy movement along patrol routes through the maze.
  • Player detection: Added line-of-sight checks to determine when an enemy can see the player.
  • Enemy pursuit: Connected player detection to chase behavior, so enemies switch from patrolling to pursuing the player when spotted.
