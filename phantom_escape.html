Phantom Escape: Enchanted Maze

Project Overview: Phantom Escape: Enchanted Maze is a captivating third-person puzzle-maze game developed in Unity. The core gameplay revolves around navigating a mysterious maze, solving puzzles, and evading intelligent AI enemies.

Key Features:

  • Advanced AI Enemy Patrols: Implemented sophisticated AI behaviors for enemies, including patrol paths, line-of-sight detection, and pursuit logic, creating a challenging and dynamic environment.
  • Seamless Animation Transitions: Designed and integrated smooth animation transitions for the player character and enemies, enhancing visual fluidity and responsiveness.
  • Immersive Atmospheric Visuals: Crafted a visually stunning environment with carefully chosen lighting, textures, and particle effects to create an enchanting yet eerie atmosphere.
